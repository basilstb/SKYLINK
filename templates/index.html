Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our global tracking, security, and settlement protocol.

How does real-time satellite telemetry work on Link Express?

Our shipments are equipped with active telemetry transponders communicating with low-earth orbit satellite networks. Coordinates, transit speed, and environmental conditions are updated directly on the live map every 30 seconds without requiring manual barcode scans.

Can I generate and print an official proof of delivery or invoice?

Yes! On the tracking page for any shipment, click "Download Receipt / PDF" to instantly generate a legally binding, high-resolution delivery receipt with full QR code verification, timestamp history, and customs duty breakdown.

What happens if a tracking number contains a typo like '0' vs 'O'?

Our system utilizes intelligent phonetic and character fault tolerance. If you accidentally type 'O' instead of '0' or omit hyphens, our query resolution engine automatically resolves and matches the correct shipment record.

Are multi-currency settlement options available for international cargo?

Yes. Through our integrated fintech clearinghouse, you can quote, invoice, and settle freight charges in USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), JPY (¥), or select institutional digital assets with guaranteed foreign exchange locks.
